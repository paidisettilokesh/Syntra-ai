Autonomous Workflow

How Syntra AI Protects Inboxes

A seamless 4-stage pipeline that monitors, verifies, and intercepts threats automatically.

STEP 01

Multi-Account Fetch

Fetches unread emails from configured Gmail OAuth/IMAP mailboxes on a background schedule.

STEP 02

Rule & Domain Check

Evaluates trusted domain JSON allowlists, sender reputation, and SPF/DKIM verification rules.

STEP 03

AI Classification

LLM categorizes email urgency, calculates numerical risk score (0-100), and flags threat vectors.

STEP 04

Instant Dispatch

Escalates suspicious emails to Telegram/WhatsApp and updates the 3D Telemetry Dashboard.

Enterprise Hardening

Security First Principles

Engineered to protect organizational data privacy while maintaining high-speed threat mitigation.

Zero Data Retention

Email body contents are processed transiently in memory for risk evaluation and never stored permanently in plain text.

Fallback LLM Security

If Groq rate limits occur, OpenRouter takes over seamlessly without dropping email verification integrity.

Automated Rule Engine

Trusted domain lists and score thresholds prevent false positives for internal organizational communications.
